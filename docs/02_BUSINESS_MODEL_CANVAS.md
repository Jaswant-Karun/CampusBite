# CampusBite — Business Model Canvas (BMC)

## Strategic E-Business Architecture for Campus Canteen Transformation

| Key Partners | Key Activities | Value Propositions | Customer Relationships | Customer Segments |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| • **College Administration**: Facility licensing, campus WiFi access, academic schedule integration.<br>• **Local Food & Beverage Suppliers**: Daily fresh produce, bakery, and dairy replenishment.<br>• **Digital Payment Infrastructure**: UPI PSPs (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), banking gateways.<br>• **Campus IT Department**: Single Sign-On (SSO) & student identity verification. | • **Food Preparation & Quality Control**: Standardized recipes and hygienic batch cooking.<br>• **Platform Operations & Order Orchestration**: Managing order dispatch and counter queuing.<br>• **Inventory & Demand Forecasting**: AI/statistical tracking of consumption to eliminate waste.<br>• **CRM & Campaign Marketing**: Launching student discount coupons and loyalty schemes. | • **For Students & Faculty**:<br>  - Zero physical queue waiting time during short lecture breaks.<br>  - Scheduled pickup slots (order during class, pick up right at bell).<br>  - Contactless simulated UPI / QR checkout.<br>  - 10% loyalty cashback and student meal coupons.<br>• **For Canteen Management**:<br>  - Real-time order book and live kitchen status tracker.<br>  - Live inventory tracking with automated low-stock warnings.<br>  - AI demand prediction for tomorrow's lunch surge.<br>  - Centralized business metrics (revenue, orders, ratings). | • **Automated Self-Service**: Intuitive smartphone app with real-time order status stepper.<br>• **Loyalty & Gamification**: Tiered rewards (*Gold Campus Diner*) with redeemable points.<br>• **Personalized Marketing**: Targeted discount coupons (`CAMPUS20`, `STUDENT10`).<br>• **Post-Order CRM Feedback**: 5-star rating and comment system for continuous quality improvement. | • **Undergraduate & Postgraduate Students**: Time-constrained between consecutive lectures.<br>• **Faculty Members & Professors**: Requiring quick lunch during 30-minute office intervals.<br>• **Campus Administrative Staff**: Regular morning tea and afternoon snack patrons.<br>• **Campus Visitors & Event Guests**: Seamless walk-up digital ordering without cash hassle. |

| Key Resources | Channels |
| :--- | :--- |
| • **Software Platform**: Express.js REST API, responsive Mobile Web App, and Executive BI Dashboard.<br>• **Kitchen Infrastructure**: Modular prep stations, designated pickup counters (Counter 1, 2, 3).<br>• **Historical Transaction Data**: Demand curves, peak rush-hour logs, and customer item preferences.<br>• **Human Capital**: Kitchen chefs, counter dispatch staff, and store manager. | • **Mobile Web / M-Commerce App**: Progressive Web App (PWA) with zero install barriers.<br>• **Campus QR Codes**: Table tents and counter stickers for instant menu access.<br>• **Campus Social Media & Notice Boards**: Announcement of exam-week promos (`EXAMSNACK`).<br>• **Digital Kitchen Display System (KDS)**: Tablet-based dispatch at counter pickup stations. |

| Cost Structure | Revenue Streams |
| :--- | :--- |
| • **Raw Material & Food Inventory**: Bulk grocery, fresh vegetables, dairy, and beverage stock.<br>• **Kitchen Operations & Labor**: Cook and counter staff wages.<br>• **Digital Infrastructure & Cloud Hosting**: Server compute, database storage, and domain SSL.<br>• **Packaging & Biodegradable Disposables**: Eco-friendly takeaway containers.<br>• **Marketing & Loyalty Redemptions**: Funded discount promotions. | • **Direct Food & Beverage Sales**: Revenue from meals, snacks, drinks, and desserts.<br>• **Convenience & Peak-Slot Priority Fees**: Optional fast-track express pickup fees.<br>• **Campus Catering & Event Packages**: Departmental meeting platters and fest catering.<br>• **Brand Promotions & Campus Sponsorships**: Partner brand sampling in student packaging. |
